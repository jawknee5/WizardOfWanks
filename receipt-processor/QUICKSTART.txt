💾 HOW IT WORKS

    ↓

    ↓
All 20+ fields extracted automatically
    ↓
User edits in real-time
    ↓
Save to local queue
    ↓
Review & approve for sync
    ↓
When online: Manual sync initiates
    ↓
Backend processes
    ↓
Updates inventory & accounting
    ↓
Complete audit trail recorded

❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Does it work offline?
A: Yes! Complete offline operation. Syncs when online.

A: App queues all transactions. Auto-syncs when POS is back.

A: Yes! Local storage only, SHA256 hashing, audit trail.

Q: Can I edit receipts?
A: Yes! Real-time on-the-fly editing with full history.

Q: Do I have to sync automatically?
